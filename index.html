Notes on building

  • How you do anything is how you do everything. A quote I heard a long time ago and it stuck with me. Reminds me of the Steve Jobs back-of-the-cabinet story. Be intentional.
  • Taste and judgement is more important than ever. Many aspects of knowledge work are becoming commoditized and as a result pattern recognition, sensitivity to nuance, and an eye for what feels right are crucial.
  • Simplicity is a competitive advantage. Work small, think big. Strip to the essential. The complicated is common; the awesomely simple is rare.
  • The work reveals itself through iteration. Measure, cut, measure again. 1% better every day. Quality emerges from selection, not perfection.
  • White space is where the real work happens. Ideas don't come from meetings or dashboards. They emerge in reflection and mental slack. Protect it like a resource.
  • The urgent destroys the important. Guard your attention & time. Most "fires" aren't.
  • Authenticity compounds. Make decisions you'd stand behind with no audience. The personal leads to the universal. Genuineness builds asymmetric trust.
  • Seek disagreement from smart people. Embed humility in all interactions. You have two ears and one mouth — use them proportionally.
  • Build systems that let ideas emerge. Promote aligned autonomy. Beginner's mind over expertise. Preconceptions limit possibilities. Create the conditions for greatness to emerge.
  • Failure is tuition. Enthusiasm is rocket fuel. It's only a failure if you didn't learn anything. And being enthusiastic is worth ~25 IQ points. Energy is contagious and underrated.

AI projects

  • Marketing Claims Analyzer Chrome extension2026

    A browser extension that brings AI-powered transparency to online shopping. It analyzes any product page — Amazon listings, Shopify stores, or any website — and evaluates every marketing claim it finds, returning a verdict of Supported, Misleading, or Unverified alongside plain-English explanations and ingredient-aware scientific reasoning. Built with a privacy-first architecture: BYOK via OpenAI or Claude, no data collection, no tracking.

    • Works on Amazon, Shopify, and any product page
    • Verdicts: Supported · Misleading · Unverified
    • Product-aware: builds ingredient context before drawing conclusions
    • BYOK via OpenAI or Claude — no data collection, no tracking
    • Fraction of a penny per analysis

    Chrome Web Store · GitHub

  • FPL Transfer Analyzer Web app2025

    A web app that gives Fantasy Premier League managers mathematically optimal transfer recommendations. Enter your team ID and it pulls your squad from the official FPL API, then solves a Mixed Integer Linear Program to maximize expected points over the next three gameweeks — accounting for budget, squad composition, club limits, selling prices, and the -4 hit for extra transfers.

    • MILP solver via PuLP + HiGHS
    • Live squad data from the official FPL API
    • Next.js 15 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS frontend
    • Python optimizer called via Next.js API routes
    • Deployed on Vercel with on-demand Python execution
